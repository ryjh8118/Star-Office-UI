RENGUIN Office · ChatGPT 工作連線

This optional local extension observes only:
- Whether a visible ChatGPT stop-generation button is present.
- The page title (shown as the planning name).
- The observation time.

It does not read messages, prompts, answers, cookies, passwords, or account data.
Observations go only to the Star Office page you paired from: Production at
http://127.0.0.1:19000 (or the Preview at http://127.0.0.1:19119). They expire
after 45 seconds. They do not update executor leases, collision safety, or
production progress.

Work in the ChatGPT desktop app is read from its local Codex records and does
not need this extension.

Installation:
1. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
2. Choose Load unpacked and select this folder.
3. Reload only the Star Office page, then open the ChatGPT member card.
4. Choose Connect ChatGPT. Existing ChatGPT pages are attached without reload.

Remove the extension in chrome://extensions, or disconnect from the Office dialog.
The installed extension requires your approval before installation.
